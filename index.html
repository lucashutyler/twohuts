About that second hut

"Two huts" started as "two Hutylers." I picked the name before I'd figured out who the second one was, and honestly, I never did.

That turned out to be the right answer. The second hut has been my father, my mother, my wife, my brothers, my children, and plenty of people who don't share my last name. A project is simply more fun with someone else in it, and finishing something is better when there's somebody to share the finish with.

So the second hut stays open. If you're working on something and want company, the door's unlocked.
