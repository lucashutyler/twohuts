Things I'm building

Each of these has other people in it, which is the point. Most are works in progress. All of them are more fun than they have any right to be.

  • Stepply

    A process management tool. Map your team's processes once, then actually run them step by step as live instances, with realtime updates and performance data on every step. Not process documentation, and not project management. The thing in between that nobody quite built.

    Side project · in progress

  • Zenosource

    A procurement platform for manufacturers: purchase orders, RFQs, price lists with quantity breaks, PO suggestions, and supplier collaboration. Deliberately opinionated. Guided workflows instead of a pile of custom fields, and every open item has somebody being reminded to act on it. Plugs into the ERP you already have.

    Side project · in progress

  • Idle Party RPG

    An idle multiplayer RPG on a hexagonal world map. Your characters fight, move, and level up whether you're online or not, and every class is stronger in a party. Built with friends on a lot of Saturdays.
