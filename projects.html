What I'm building

Each of these has somebody else in it, on purpose. Most are works in progress. All of them are more fun than they have any right to be.

  • Stepply

    A process management tool. Map your team's processes once, then actually run them step by step, with live updates and performance data on every step. Not process documentation, and not project management. The thing in between that nobody quite built.

    Side project · in progress

  • Zenosource

    A procurement platform for manufacturers: purchase orders, RFQs, price lists with quantity breaks, PO suggestions, and supplier collaboration. It has opinions. Guided workflows instead of a pile of custom fields, and nothing sits open without somebody getting a reminder. Plugs into the ERP you already have.

    Side project · in progress

  • Idle Party RPG

    An idle multiplayer RPG on a hexagonal world map. Your characters fight, move, and level up whether you're online or not, and every class is stronger in a party. Built with friends on a lot of Saturdays.

    Side project · play.idlepartyrpg.com

  • Amphenol Network Solutions

    My day job, where I'm the Director of IT. Over the years that has meant the website, internal applications, SQL and ETL pipelines, reporting, and lately the software behind the next generation of nrgSMART power distribution panels.

    Day job · nrgSMART panels
